# MedWatch — 2-Minute Team Demo Script

Live URL: https://manipal-hackathon-medwatch.vercel.app
One person drives the screen (whoever's most comfortable clicking live) while the others narrate their part — practice the handoffs twice so nobody talks over a click.

**Speaking time:** Priyal opens and carries the core insight; Tanvi carries redistribution + AI and closes with Priyal — together they hold the bulk of the video (~90s). Dinesh and Allwin have one short, punchy handoff in the middle (~15s).

Reset the simulation (top-right button) before recording so you start at Day 0.

---

## 0:00–0:45 — Priyal (opening + the core insight)

Screen: full dashboard, Day 0.

> "A health facility running low on one medicine looks like routine noise — until nearby facilities run low on the *same* one, at the *same* time. That's the pattern MedWatch catches early."

Click into a facility detail drawer briefly, then point at the Regional Risk Alerts panel.

> "Every facility forecasts its own stockout date with a confidence score — never overstating certainty. Three real facilities in Kundapura Taluk are all trending toward the same Amoxicillin stockout within days of each other — that's a **Regional Risk**, not a guess. One facility alone in Udupi Taluk, low on Insulin? That's an **Isolated Event**. It even reads *why* — a demand spike, or a supply delay — because that changes what to actually do."

Point at the Projected Risk Trend chart.

> "And this curve shows how that local signal could grow into a wider disruption over the next 20 days if nobody acts."

---

## 0:45–1:00 — Dinesh + Allwin (middle handoff)

Screen: stays on the dashboard, maybe a slow pan to the facility map.

**Dinesh:** "It's built on real government facilities in Udupi district, Karnataka —"
**Allwin:** "— real names, real GPS, real distances. Not a toy dataset."

---

## 1:00–1:45 — Tanvi (redistribution + AI)

Point at the Redistribution Suggestions panel and the map's arrows.

> "Once a shortage is flagged, MedWatch finds the nearest facility with real surplus and recommends a transfer — real distance, real impact: without this, stockout in 11 days; with it, pushed to 18. Nothing here is a black box.
>
> And there's a real AI layer on top — Ask MedWatch. Type a question in plain English, like 'which facility needs urgent action right now,' and it answers using only the numbers the engine already computed. It can't invent a fact that isn't there."

Click one suggested question in the Ask MedWatch panel if time allows; otherwise just point at it.

---

## 1:45–2:00 — Priyal + Tanvi (close together)

**Priyal:** "Real facilities, a transparent engine —"
**Tanvi:** "— and AI that explains, not replaces. That's MedWatch."

End on the full dashboard, Day 0.

---

## Notes for whoever's driving the screen

- Keep the slider moves (if any) in the Day 0–10 range — past ~day 15 extra noise appears from simulated data, not worth it in a 2-minute cut.
- If a "Why?" popover doesn't close between clicks, click any blank space first.
- Full-screen the browser (hide the URL bar / bookmarks) before recording.
- Rehearse the two handoffs (Priyal→Dinesh/Allwin, Allwin→Tanvi) out loud once — those are the only places timing can slip.
