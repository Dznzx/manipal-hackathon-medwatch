# MedWatch — 2-Minute Team Demo Script

**Live app:** https://manipal-hackathon-medwatch.vercel.app

One person drives the screen (whoever's most comfortable clicking live) while the others narrate their part. Rehearse the three handoffs out loud at least twice — that's the only place timing can slip.

**Speaking time:** Priyal 40s · Dinesh 20s · Allwin 20s · Tanvi 40s. Total 2:00.

Reset the simulation (top-right button) before recording so you start at Day 0.

---

## 0:00–0:40 — Priyal (opening + the core insight)

Screen: full dashboard, Day 0.

> "A health facility running low on one medicine looks like routine noise — until nearby facilities run low on the *same* one, at the *same* time. MedWatch catches that pattern early.
>
> Every facility forecasts its own stockout date with a confidence score — never overstating certainty. Three real facilities in Kundapura Taluk are all trending toward the same Amoxicillin stockout within days of each other — that's a **Regional Risk**, not a guess. One facility alone in Udupi Taluk, low on Insulin? That's an **Isolated Event**. It even reads *why* it's happening — a demand spike, or a supply delay."

---

## 0:40–1:00 — Dinesh

Screen: point at the facility map / facility list.

> "And this isn't a made-up map. These are 11 real government PHCs, CHCs, and hospitals in Udupi district, Karnataka — real names, real GPS coordinates, real distances between them. Only the stock numbers are simulated, because that data simply isn't public anywhere."

---

## 1:00–1:20 — Allwin

Screen: point at the Projected Risk Trend chart, then toward the Redistribution panel.

> "This curve shows how today's signal could grow into a wider disruption over the next 20 days if nobody acts. So the next question is obvious — is there surplus somewhere nearby that could help?"

---

## 1:20–2:00 — Tanvi (redistribution + AI + close)

Point at the Redistribution Suggestions panel and the map's arrows.

> "MedWatch finds the nearest facility with real surplus and recommends a transfer — real distance, real impact: without this, stockout in 11 days; with it, pushed to 18. Nothing here is a black box.
>
> There's a real AI layer on top too — Ask MedWatch. Type a question in plain English, and it answers using only the numbers the engine already computed. It can't invent a fact that isn't there.
>
> Real facilities, a transparent engine, and AI that explains, not replaces. That's MedWatch."

End on the full dashboard, Day 0.

---

## Notes for whoever's driving the screen

- Keep any slider moves in the Day 0–10 range — past ~day 15, extra noise appears from simulated data, not worth it in a 2-minute cut.
- If a "Why?" popover doesn't close between clicks, click any blank space first.
- Full-screen the browser (hide the URL bar / bookmarks) before recording.
- Rehearse the three handoffs (Priyal→Dinesh, Dinesh→Allwin, Allwin→Tanvi) out loud once each.
